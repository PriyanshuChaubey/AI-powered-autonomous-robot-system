Build a two-wheeled ESP32 robot that drives forward, measures distance ahead
with an ultrasonic sensor, and stops and turns before hitting an obstacle.
Use an L298N motor driver, two IR obstacle sensors for side awareness, and a
status LED. The control firmware must be generated by our AI pipeline, stream
JSON telemetry over serial at 115200 baud, and run a non-blocking control loop
at about 50 Hz.
